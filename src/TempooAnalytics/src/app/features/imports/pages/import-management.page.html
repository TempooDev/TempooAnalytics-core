Plausible Analytics

Upload the export .zip from Plausible

  1. In Plausible, open your site and go to Site settings → Imports & Exports.
  2. Click Export data and download the .zip from the email Plausible sends you.
  3. Upload that .zip here. We import daily visitors, pageviews, bounce rate and visit duration, plus top pages, sources, countries, devices, browsers and operating systems.

Imported days are only used before the first day Tempoo recorded traffic for this site, so nothing is counted twice. Importing again replaces the previous import.
